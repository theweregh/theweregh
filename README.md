## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🌱 Terrafy

**Smart Farming · IoT · Mobile Development · Data Monitoring**

Mobile application designed for monitoring and managing agronomic and environmental variables in NFT hydroponic crop systems.

* Monitoring of temperature, humidity, pH, and electrical conductivity.
* IoT/IoUT sensor data collection modeling and simulation.
* Historical data management and graphical analysis.
* Designed to reduce manual intervention and support continuous crop monitoring.

---

### ⚔️ Nexus Battles IV: A New Hope

**Cloud · Web Development · Multiplayer Systems · E-Commerce · AI**

Cloud-hosted, web-based turn-based RPG featuring both PvP and PvE systems.

* Character progression, quests, tournaments, and inventory management.
* Online auction system supporting item trading through credits or real money.
* Bidding and instant-purchase functionality.
* AI-powered chatbot for customer support.
* Notification system for auctions, tournaments, missions, and other events.

---

### 🚆 RailwaysGranCol

**Java · MVC · Linux · Transportation Management**

Java-based transportation management system developed following the **Model-View-Controller (MVC)** architectural pattern and deployed in a Linux environment.

* Passenger and train management.
* Ticket purchasing and validation.
* Baggage management.
* User, train, and route administration.
* Digitalization of previously manual data-collection processes.

---

### 🎓 SAFI

**Academic Management · Student Support**

Management system developed to support academic follow-up and student support processes.

* Student progress tracking.
* Academic information management.
* Support for institutional formation and monitoring processes.

---

### 🚗 DriveZone — AUTO-UPB Consortium

**Spring Boot · Scrum · CRM/ERP Integration · Enterprise Systems**

Software integration project developed as part of the **AUTO-UPB consortium**, involving five automotive companies in Bucaramanga.

* Integration of CRM and ERP environments.
* Interoperability and data exchange between consortium members.
* Support for vehicle, parts, accessories, customer service, and maintenance operations.
* Online accessories store for DriveZone customers.
* Developed using **Spring Boot** under the **Scrum** framework.
* Experience with sprint planning, logs, iterative development, and delivery.
